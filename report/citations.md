# Citation pointers for the Math 195 report

Checked 2026-10-19. BibTeX entries are in `refs.bib` in this folder. Keys use the Author:Year form.

**Confidence levels**
- **VERIFIED**: I saw the pointer in the text, or in the publisher's table of contents for chapter and section numbers.
- **VERIFIED (bib)**: the bibliographic data was checked and no pointer was asked for.
- **PARTLY**: the bibliographic data was verified, but the pointer was not, or only in a different version of the work.
- **UNVERIFIED**: not checked.

**How the bibliographic data was checked**
- Every DOI in `refs.bib` was resolved against api.crossref.org.
- Books without a DOI were checked against publisher pages or tables of contents.

**Versions used for page numbers**
- **ISLP**: the free PDF from statlearning.com, marked "First Printing: July 5, 2023". All pages cited are the book's printed page numbers, taken from the running heads.
- **ESL**: the free PDF from hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn, 2nd ed., "Corrected 12th printing - Jan 13, 2017". Printed page numbers.
- **Other works**: where a pointer was verified only in an author, preprint or working-paper version, the row says so.
